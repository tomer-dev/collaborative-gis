## Gaps and honest limitations

1. Viewport reads stop at the 500 most recently updated areas of the snapped tile, with no truncation flag, paging, clustering or zoom simplification (`zoom` is ignored), so a dense tile silently loses areas.
2. The 50-per-minute limit counts REST mutations and draft sessions, not frames (D5). One tab can send up to 600 draft frames a minute under the separate 10/s-per-socket cap. A strict reader may count each vertex as a "drawing action".
3. The Room writes nothing to `audit_log`: joins, leaves, presence changes, refused or unsaved draft sessions and WS `RATE_LIMITED` errors leave no per-user record. `audit_log` also has no index and no retention, so it grows forever.
4. Realtime is one Room DO for the whole app (D11), with synchronous fan-out and no message queue. Load was measured only locally against `wrangler dev`, at 10 REST users and 5 sockets, never against production or at the 50/100 users the spec's §8 planned.
5. Aerial imagery depends on govmap's legacy cache (newest tiles 2024-01), which govmap can retire without notice, and there is no fallback (D3). The aerial e2e specs need the internet and are excluded from CI.
6. docker-compose covered the database only at audit time; since PR #67 (merged 2026-10-13, verified with a real run) it also runs the API, whose Durable Object state is lost when the container is recreated.
7. "No third-party plugins for the collaborative features" is read narrowly (D25): drawing uses `leaflet-editable`, and area and kinks use `@turf/*`. A reviewer who counts drawing as collaborative would disagree.
8. Switching base layers can change the zoom level after a round trip (z15 → L07 → z16; z0–z8 → L00 → z7); the crossfade's old-layer snapshot is removed when the animation ends, not when the new tiles have loaded (feature check at 267deb2).
9. Production-side controls (WAF rule, Cloudflare Health Check and its alert) are dashboard steps that the repository does not show. A revoked session lags up to 5 minutes (D24). The sign-in limiter counts per isolate. Deploying needs Workers Paid.
10. Known consistency windows: an area event between a viewport's first GET and the socket's join, or a read overlapping a write before its version bump, can miss that write until the next pan or reconnect. Cross-region Room placement is untested.
